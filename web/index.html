Controls

Co-op: Player 2 presses B in game to join (and rides in Crash's backpack, Y to jump off). Two gamepads: the second one is Player 2.

Click a key, then press the new one (Esc cancels). Enter is always A too.

Click an input, then press the gamepad button for it (Esc cancels). The sticks stay as they are.

Online co-op

Play the story together, each in their own browser, through this server. The host plays as usual (a new game goes to the HUB); the others join and follow the host into every level it starts. Up to 4 players.

Sessions on this server

Looking...

Joining while the host is in a level (back after a drop, too): you wait at the main menu and come in at the host's next level. Only the host keeps a save.
